# FCP Authoring Rules

Status: FROZEN

1. Flashcard fronts are concepts, not questions.
2. Do not manufacture cards to satisfy a fixed quantity.
3. Create a concept inventory before drafting cards.
4. Merge synonyms and abbreviations into one semantic identity where appropriate.
5. Preserve one Concept -> one canonical Flashcard in FC V1.
6. Definitions must be concise, technically accurate, and source-faithful.
7. Why It Matters must add practical or exam-relevant meaning rather than repeat the definition.
8. Key Point is optional and must stay compact.
9. Use 2–8 canonical tags.
10. Every learner-ready factual card requires valid provenance.
11. Safety-critical numbers must retain unit, condition, jurisdiction, and source context.
12. Formula cards explain the relationship and use. They are not calculation questions.
13. Do not copy proprietary standards verbatim without explicit permitted-use status.
14. If the meaning or source cannot be demonstrated, block the card.
