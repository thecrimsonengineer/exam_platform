## Purpose

ML-9E is the human-review boundary for the 120-fact MicroFact candidate corpus.

Automation may prepare review packets, validate their structure, and enforce fail-closed rules. It must not create or infer human approvals.

## Review package

The corpus is divided into 12 packets of 10 facts:

```text
content/micro_learning/human_review/
  ml9e_batch_01_human_review.json
  ...
  ml9e_batch_12_human_review.json
```

Each fact requires review of:

- technical accuracy and context;
- source identity, locator, currency, and claim support;
- rights treatment and independent wording;
- curriculum alignment when the fact is mapped;
- pedagogy and precision;
- accessibility;
- learner-facing UI suitability.

General-scope CSP reasoning facts use `not_applicable` only for curriculum review.

## Human attestation rule

A channel may be marked `pass` only when a real reviewer explicitly supplies:

- `reviewerId`;
- an allowed `reviewerRole`;
- `humanAttested: true`;
- a non-empty rationale;
- `reviewedAt`;
- `nextReviewDueAt`;
- every item in `requiredChecks` inside `confirmedChecks`.

A `pass` with missing human identity, dates, rationale, or checks is invalid.

Review validity may not exceed 365 days.

## Fact decision

Use:

```text
pending  -> review is not finished
accept   -> every applicable channel passed
revise   -> at least one channel failed and the fact needs correction
reject   -> at least one channel failed and the fact should not proceed
```

`revise` and `reject` require `revisionNotes`.

## Batch completion

A packet can use:

```json
"reviewStatus": "completed",
"humanReviewComplete": true
```

only when all 10 facts have `overallDecision: "accept"`.

## Corpus completion

ML-9E is complete only when:

- all 12 packets are completed;
- all 120 facts are accepted;
- all applicable review channels are human-attested pass;
- the ML-9E manifest is changed to `status: "completed"`;
- the ML-9E manifest has `humanReviewComplete: true`;
- the complete validator passes.

## Validation

Review-package readiness:

```powershell
dart run tool/validate_ml9e_human_review.dart
```

Final human-review completion:

```powershell
dart run tool/validate_ml9e_human_review.dart --require-complete
```

The second command is expected to fail until genuine human review is complete.
